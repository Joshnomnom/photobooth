# 02 · Competitor landscape (web photo booths)

Surveyed 2026-11-27. Feature claims are taken from each product's own pages and haven't been tested hands-on.

| Product | What it does | Takeaway |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Flashback Booth](https://www.producthunt.com/products/flashback-booth?embed=true) | Strips, film filters, custom date stamps, processed 100% locally | Closest to our current build. Date stamps and "private" are baseline. |
| [Groovy Photo Booth](https://www.framer.com/community/contests/agents-hackathon/submissions/LPb3GWv26C88GHREQau9T7/) | 1970s-themed booth, no app, no uploads, no sign-up | A single strong era theme works. |

| Native: Pocketbooth (iOS) | Strip-based booth app | Has been around a long time. |

## What everyone already has (the baseline)

- 3–4 shot strips, a countdown, filters
- Film grain, light leaks, date stamps
- "Private, in your browser, no sign-up"
- One-click download

## What nobody does well (our opening)

1. **The physical ritual.** Curtain, coin or start lever, flash, strip printing out of a slot, developing.
2. **A coherent era identity.** Most offer a filter list. Few make one whole machine (look, paper, type, sound, motion) feel like a real era.
3. **Craft in the film look.** Most grain is uniform noise. Grain that follows brightness, highlight glow, and slight frame-to-frame variation read as *real*.
4. **The keepsake afterlife.** A scrapbook or wall of past strips that persists on your device.
5. **Decorating.** Purikura-style play is rare on the web.

## Positioning statement (draft)

> **The Snap Room** is the web photo booth that feels like stepping into a real one: pick a machine from another decade, pose through the flashes, and watch your strip develop in your hands. Private, instant, and a little bit magic.
